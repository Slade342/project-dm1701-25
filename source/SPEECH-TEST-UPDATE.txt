WINDOWS P25 SPEECH TEST UPDATE

Extract this ZIP, then copy its DM1701-Studio folder contents into your existing DM1701-Studio folder, replacing files.
Double-click Test-P25-Speech.cmd. Python must already be installed. No USB connection or firmware flashing is needed.
Expected: a 5.22-second spoken test plays on your PC. WAV and JSON report: firmware-p25/voice/results.
The Windows x64 decoder was cross-compiled; the equivalent Linux decoder passed the fixture test. Windows execution still needs your test.
This does not enable P25 audio on the radio. Raw SAVE.jsonl captures are not yet demodulated P25 voice bits.
For receive-path diagnostics use the CPS P25 lab receive test. Read P25-RECEIVE-TESTS.md for RX-only frequencies.
